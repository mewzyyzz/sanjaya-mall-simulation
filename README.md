# Sanjaya Mall Simulation

A simple, interactive console-based shopping mall simulation program written in C. Explore multiple stores, manage your shopping cart, and calculate your total purchases—all entirely in English.

## Features
* **Multiple Stores:** Visit four distinct stores offering a wide variety of items:
  * **Stationery Store:** Notebook, Pen Set, Backpack, and Ruler.
  * **Food Store:** Fried Rice, Fried Noodles, Fried Chicken, and Meatball.
  * **Drink Store:** Mineral Water, Tea, Coca Cola, and Orange Juice.
  * **Toy Store:** Lego, Hotwheels, Pokemon Card, and Rubik's Cube.
* **Interactive Cart & Subtotals:** Add custom quantities of items to your cart, view individual subtotals, and track your ongoing spending in real-time.
* **Clean Console Interface:** Structured text layout with user-friendly error handling for invalid menu choices.

## How to Run
1. Make sure you have a C compiler installed (such as GCC).

   gcc main.c -o sanjaya_mall
